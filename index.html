Official Export Publication

Cirebon Rattan Heritage: Time-Honored Weaving Artistry for Global Interiors

Led by Mr. H. Ridwan Mansyur, B.Eng., our enterprise operates directly from the historic epicenter of global rattan manufacturing: Tegalwangi, Weru, Cirebon—a region renowned for processing over 80% of the world's premium rattan with generational craftsmanship.

Established in 2008, our company was founded on Mr. Ridwan's deep commitment to preserving Cirebon's weaving legacy while meeting the stringent durability standards of international commercial hospitality. Combining industrial engineering rigor with the artistry of over 120 master craftsmen, we have evolved into a trusted export partner for luxury developments worldwide.

We selectively harvest solid Manau, Tohiti, and Mandola rattan poles from certified sustainable conservation forests in Kalimantan and Sulawesi. Every piece undergoes rigorous high-temperature steam bending, kiln drying to a strict Moisture Content (MC < 12%), anti-pest treatments, and multi-tier structural quality checks.
